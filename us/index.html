Legacy Platforms

Edge+ is a unified compliance platform built for financial institutions, integrating key regulatory functions to improve efficiency and reduce risk. By centralizing client onboarding, payment screening, and post-transaction monitoring, it strengthens adherence to AML and financial regulations. With automated workflows, real-time screening, and continuous monitoring, Edge+ enhances operational control and supports smarter, compliant decision-making across regulated teams.

The HBusiness platform is a secure online portal meticulously designed for streamlined business management. It enables users to conveniently access their accounts, manage transactions, and retrieve internal analysis data. This functionality ensures operational efficiency and enhances financial oversight, effectively catering to the diverse needs of businesses of all sizes.

QoR3+ is a comprehensive core system built to streamline and optimize workflows across Payments, Foreign Exchange Operations, and Finance departments. Users benefit from fully customizable accounting reports, an automated revaluation function, and streamlined bank account reconciliation for enhanced accuracy and transparency. QoR3+ empowers users to operate with greater control, agility, and confidence in a global financial environment.

Ficoy+, our HRMS, is a strategic platform that integrates all people-centric processes into one seamless ecosystem and streamlines the entire employee lifecycle—from recruitment and onboarding to performance management, payroll, and exits. It centralizes all employment data, empowers teams through self-service, and enables leaders with insights needed to build a thriving workforce.

Coventrix+ is a centralized platform that integrates all departmental information, enhancing collaboration and communication. With tools for project management and real-time analytics, teams can make data-driven decisions. Customizable dashboards and robust security improve productivity and transparency, ensuring alignment with company goals across Sales, finance, marketing, and operations and other departments.

Digiqore+ is a comprehensive platform designed to automate and optimize payment workflows through advanced routing logic, enabling seamless transaction processing without manual intervention. It allows users to configure bank charges dynamically based on amount, currency, or bank, and supports comprehensive STP (Straight Through Processing) rules aligned with SWIFT standards and individual bank protocols. With global bank routing management, DigiQore empowers ensuring efficient, compliant, and cost-effective cross-border payments.

HMarkets is a platform designed to facilitate the FX Conversion for cross border payments with speed and precision. It supports major currency pairs and provides comprehensive reporting tools, including currency-wise transaction summaries and records of booked and cancelled trades.
